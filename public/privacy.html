Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

UniPath ("we," "us," or "our") helps students apply to universities in the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, and Australia. This policy explains what information we collect through unipathedu.org and our booking process, how we use it, and the choices you have.

01Information we collect

When you book a consultation or purchase a package through our checkout flow, we collect the information you provide directly, including:

  • Full name, email address, and phone number
  • Your destination country and target intake term
  • Any notes or goals you share with your mentor
  • The package or service you selected

We do not collect payment card details ourselves — see Payment processing below.

02How we use your information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Deliver the consultation, review, or coaching service you booked
  • Match you with a mentor suited to your destination and goals
  • Send booking confirmations and respond to your questions
  • Improve our services and communications

We do not use your information for automated decision-making, and we do not sell it to third parties.

03Payment processing

All payments are processed by Stripe, Inc., a PCI-compliant third-party payment processor. When you check out, you're redirected to Stripe's own secure payment page — your card number, expiry, and CVC are entered directly with Stripe and never pass through or touch our servers. We receive only confirmation that a payment succeeded, the amount charged, and the billing email you provided to Stripe.

04Cookies & tracking

Our website does not currently use advertising or analytics cookies. Stripe's checkout page sets its own cookies as part of fraud prevention and payment processing, governed by Stripe's Privacy Policy.

05Data sharing & disclosure

We share your information only as needed to run our business:

  • With Stripe, to process your payment
  • With your assigned mentor, so they can prepare for your session
  • When required by law, such as in response to a valid legal request

We never sell or rent your personal information to advertisers or data brokers.

06Data retention

We keep your information for as long as needed to provide our services and to meet legal, accounting, or reporting obligations — typically for the duration of your engagement with a mentor plus a reasonable follow-up period. You can request earlier deletion at any time (see Your rights).

07Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to object to certain uses of it. To exercise any of these rights, email us at unipatheducationkh@gmail.com — we'll respond within a reasonable time.

08Children's privacy

Our services are intended for prospective university students and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

09Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
